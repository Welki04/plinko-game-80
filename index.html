    How PLINKO works from start to finish

    Every round of PLINKO follows a clean sequence. You choose a stake, set the board style or risk level if the site allows it, and let the ball drop. As it moves through the peg field, it lands in a slot that determines the payout for that round. In a proper plinko online setup, the result is fixed by the game engine before the ball finishes its path, so nothing you click midway changes the outcome.

    That is why plinko betting is mostly about planning before the round starts. The result is not shaped by the last drop, the previous slot, or a pattern in the board. Each round stands on its own, which is why players in a plinko live casino or standard online version usually focus on stake control instead of trying to read streaks.
